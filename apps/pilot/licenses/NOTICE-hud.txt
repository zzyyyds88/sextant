chopin-priv-hud
===============

The HUD's status capsule and stop control implement the agent-control status
surface documented by the dsh-go project:

  https://github.com/ccch1mneyyy/dsh-go
  docs/design/phone-overlay.md

dsh-go's phone overlay is built around AccessibilityService
TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY windows. This HUD is a separate Java implementation
for a module-owned SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW APK; it does not copy the dsh-go Flutter
application, its accessibility service, or its window-management code.
